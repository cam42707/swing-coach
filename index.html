Best recording setup

Angle
HeightHip to belt height (about 3 ft). Phone level, not tilted.
Distance10–12 ft away (4 big steps). Whole body, club at the top, and a little space above your head and below your feet in frame. Keep the phone upright.
LightingLight on you, behind the camera. Never sun or a bright window behind you. Bright, even light — dim light blurs the swing.
SteadyUse a tripod or prop the phone. Same spot every time so swings compare fairly.
ExtrasPlain background, fitted clothes. Hold still 1 sec at address and hold your finish 1 sec.

Settings

Stored only on this phone. Only still frames + numbers are sent to Claude, never the video.
